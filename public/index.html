Auction Draft Rules

  • Auction Budget: $200 per team ($2,400 total across 12 teams).
  • Roster Size: 15 total players (9 starting lineup, 6 reserves/bench).
  • Starting Lineup: 1 QB, 2 RB, 2 WR, 2 Flex (RB/WR/TE), 1 K, 1 DST.
  • Max Bid Rule: Owners cannot submit a bid that leaves a balance less than $1 per open position on the starting roster.
  • Forfeiture: Any remaining unspent balance at the end of the draft is forfeited and is NOT rolled over into waivers.
  • Nomination: Serpentine order. Submitting owner provides opening bid, player name, team, and position.
